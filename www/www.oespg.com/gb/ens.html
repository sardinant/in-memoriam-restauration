In addition to a wide range of activities, the Scouts Association uses traditional theological teaching practices based on religious devotion, the assimilation of the key values of Catholicism and personal development.

Lessons are punctuated with various courses that scouts are invited to attend, depending on their age and merit:

Christian Training Course
The objective of this course is for scouts to understand the fundamentals of catechism and the main principles of moral judgement. Learning to live according to the Christian tradition requires a full understanding of the sacraments, festivals and dogma. This initial course is both essential and compulsory for every new scout.

Prayer Vigil
Taking part in the prayer vigils enables a close link to be maintained with God through processions. This is the ultimate devout reward for our scouts, the most deserving of whom will be allowed to say the prayer and help to organise mass.

Pilgrimages
Advancing towards a meeting with Christ will be available to the deserving scout who has successfully overcome all the trials.

Retreats
For an absolute spiritual accomplishment, the Scouts Association invites the youngsters to a retreat, so that they can move intimately close to Christ and pledge their loyalty.
