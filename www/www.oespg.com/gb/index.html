The St Paul's Scouts Association founded by Father Malard is aimed at the intellectual, moral and physical development of boys aged between 6 and 16.

Our structure is based on spiritual development, physical effort and the concept of respect for essential values, such as the family, sharing, perseverance, endurance and discipline.

The sports activities and traditional teaching practices allow the children to take part in rewarding events, thereby helping to develop their sense of duty, rigour and courage, and enabling them to maintain a good level of health for serving the divine will.
