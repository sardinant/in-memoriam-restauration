The Maccioni cement works were founded in 1875 by Filipo Maccioni and today are firmly established as the leader in the Italian public buildings and works sector.

The plant is located on the outskirts of Naples and produces 750,000 tons of cement for building contractors and firms.
The combination of its wide range of products and its traditional expertise has made Maccioni the leading producer of cement in Italy.

The company always has an ear turned towards its customers and their requirements, and its engineering team is ready to implement its experience within the production sector, which explains why the plant offers a wide range of cement adapted to various uses: highway and road works, new accommodation, restoration projects, public buildings, drainage, works of art, and so on.

The constantly updated equipment and processes at the Maccioni cement works always reflect the latest cutting-edge technology, thereby guaranteeing its customers high quality products. Its key words are durability, resistance, diversity and preservation of the environment.
