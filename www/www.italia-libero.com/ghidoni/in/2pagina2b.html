You can admire the magnificent Annunciation by Joos Amman von Ravensburg and a rather morbid painting depicting the Dominican monk and inquisitor St Peter of Verona with a knife protruding from his head, a sign of the punishment inflicted by the heretics. Do not miss the famous Crocifisso Miracoloso to the left of the altar - according to the legend, Christ's head moved to warn a young woman of her lover's treachery!
