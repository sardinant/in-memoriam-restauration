Scholarships
     

To promote research in the fields of epigraphy and codicology, the Volker Institute awards scholarships every year of 1,200 euros per month. These scholarships are intended for young researchers who already hold an MA and are working towards a PhD or undertaking postdoctoral research, and who are working in the fields covered by the institute. Application forms must be sent to the Board of Directors before 30 June every year at the following address:
     
 
Volker Institute

Secretarial Offices
Friedrichstrasse 95
55127 Mainz
     
 
Or by email: information@volker-institut.com
 
Scholarship applications must contain:
  •

A letter from the applicant addressed to the director of the relevant research unit
  • An information sheet
  • An outline of a specific research project
  • A letter of recommendation from the thesis supervisor

You can download the scholarship application form
(in Word 97 format)
 
   
Volker Institute Prize
The institute also organises an annual competition, with prizes going to a thesis from each research unit.
