The Volker Institute decided to concentrate research activities around three complementary units, each under the supervision of a Scientific Director.

Laboratory for Epigraphy, Codicology and Papyrology
Prof. Vera Lebina

Research Subjects
Processing of epigraphic data to contribute towards comparative research in ancient worlds.

Current Projects
Editing and publication of Greek inscriptions discovered since 1945.
In-depth and methodological research in sources in ancient history.
Creation of the Restoration centre, implementing the latest equipment for effectively treating recent discoveries.

      Associates
University of Mainz - Department of Ancient Palaeography.
 
 
  Comparative Research Centre for Ancient Cultures
Prof. Jan Babureck

Research Subjects
Analysis of cultural structures in the Mediterranean world from its origins to the Roman conquest, collection of literary and epigraphic sources, papyri and codices, archaeological research in cultural buildings, such as ancient libraries, for the purpose of studying the transmission of culture by ancient civilisations and gauging the concept of patrimony in antiquity. A sub-unit is specifically focused on the history of books and writing.
     

Current Projects
Comparative research in the concept of culture through the Mediterranean world. Creation of a general directory of epigraphic sources for cultural history. Structure and readership of ancient libraries: research into the cultural contents.

    Research Centre for Ancient Philosophies
Prof. Viktor Reinhart

Research Subjects
Provide philologists, religion-oriented historians and anthropologists with a corpus of texts, so that they can be commented on from the theological and anthropological point of view. Propose new translations from texts written in their source language and establish a set of themes. Analysis of cultural relations between the East and the religious and philosophical trends in the Mediterranean basin.
Current Projects
Study of Plato's influence during the first few centuries AC.
