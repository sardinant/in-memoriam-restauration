The Volker Institute was created in 1965 for the purpose of promoting archaeological and palaeographic research in the Mediterranean basin.

The institute is specialised in the study of the languages and culture of ancient worlds and has developed tightly-knit research units in close collaboration with the biggest names in research and with backing from the Ministry for the Arts and Education. 
The institute has become a meeting place open to all students, researchers and lecturers wishing to advance the knowledge of the Mediterranean world from its origins to late antiquity. That is why the institute is equipped with specialist equipment, an exhaustive database of Greek and Latin inscriptions, papyri and codices, and an extensive library containing more than 500 specialist works and reviews.
