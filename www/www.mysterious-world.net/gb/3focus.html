For almost 4,000 years, Salisbury Plain has been home to a set of stones pointing towards the sky and whose origin and purpose remain a real enigma.
   
Stonehenge dates back to the Neolithic period and the Bronze Age, and contains 4 concentric circles, surrounded by a ditch and an embankment pierced by some 56 wells.
   

From historical interpretations to alien theories, Mysterious World is ready to give you the latest on prehistoric England's legendary site.

1) Stonehenge - the work of giants from Mars?

Stonehenge has always been the subject of controversial theories, what with the legend going that this spectacular stone structure was the work of giants and was moved to the southwest of England by Merlin the magician as part of some funeral ceremony.

The work of the Egyptians? A Celtic shrine? A Greek or Maya construction? Some even think it's a temple dedicated to Apollo, built by the people of Atlantis described by Plato.

And what if Stonehenge was simply a landmark for extraterrestrials? A study carried out by Professor David Percy compares the photos taken of the Martian site of Cydonia with Stonehenge. By using a set of transparencies, the scientist has reported stunning similarities between the two perspectives, in particular, the location of the spiral abutment at Cydonia is a perfect match with the Stonehenge circle! This extraordinary phenomenon has also been noticed with the megalithic site of Glastonbury, whose Tor corresponds to the peak of the 5-sided pyramid seen on Mars.

2) From the astronomical function to extraterrestrial purgatory

Whereas astronomers saw the stones as some sort of calendar on a supernatural dimension and historians believed it to be a druid temple for human sacrifices, this discovery has definitely set the cat among the pigeons.
 

To lend weight to the theory of Stonehenge representing a work by extraterrestrials, you need to look at the other paranormal phenomenon of crop circles, which appear near to megalithic sites, as is the case a few kilometres from Stonehenge. These geometric symbols found in crop fields are actually considered to be messages from extraterrestrials.

We ought to point out that these strange sites also contain uranium and high levels of radioactivity, which gives a real Roswell feel to the prehistoric site of Stonehenge!
   

So what was its exact purpose? A compulsory halfway house for visitors from out of space? A terrestrial observatory? A base? Purgatory?

The question has yet to be answered.
