J’ai pour ma part quelques éléments bibliographiques concernant ce sujet :

ADLER, Kraig. Loch Ness monster evidence presented at Cornell University. 1976 Herpetological Review, 7 [n° 2] : 41-46 (June).

BOYD, Alastair, and David MARTIN Creating a monster. 1994.BBC Wildlife, 12 [n° 4] : 22-23 (April).

COSTELLO, Peter In search of lake monsters. 1974.New York, Coward, McCann and Geoghegan.

MACKAL, Roy P. The monsters of Loch Ness.1976. Chicago, Swallow Press.

RAZDAN, Rikki, and Alan KIELAR Sonar and photographic searches for the Loch Ness Monster : a reassessment. In FRAZIER, Kendrick, Science Confronts the Paranormal, 1986.New York, Prometheus Books : 349-358.
