CREATING THE MAGIC CIRCLE

Prior to summoning any spirits, you must create the magic circle in which the entire force of the operation will be concentrated and which will provide protection from the evil spirits. It varies according to the spirits to be summoned, the place, the season, the day and the hour. It comprises three concentric circles spaced one palm apart (7.36 cm), the first of which with a 9-foot diameter (2.65 m).

In the middle circle, you must write:

1. The name of the hour at which the conjuration begins
2. The name of the angel of the hour
3. The sigil of the angel of the hour
4. The name of the angel ruling the day in which the conjuration is performed and the names of his ministers
5. The name of the season
6. The name of the sprits ruling in that season and their Presidents
7. The name of the head of the sign ruling in that season
8. The name of the earth in that season
9. The name of the sun and the moon in that season

In the outer circle, you must write:

1. The names of the presidential angels of the air on the day on which the conjuration is performed
meaning the name of the king and his three ministers.

In the inner circle, you must write:

1. The four divine names, separated by a cross

In the middle of the circle, you must write:

1. Alpha on the east side, and Omega on the west side

Finally, you must draw a cross to divide the middle of the circle so that it is perfect, such as shown below:


Illustration of the magic circle for the first hour of Sunday in spring

There is a hierarchical order in which all these names are to be presented. The angels rule the hours in successive order according to the course of the heavens and the planets (days) over which they also rule, so that the spirit ruling the day also governs the first hour of the day; the second spirit of the day governs the second hour, the third spirit governs the third hour, and so on. When the seven days and seven hours have passed, it returns again to the first ruling the day. To the magician attempting this operation, the divine names (first circle) give power over the world of angels, the angelic names (second circle) give power over the world of spirits and the names of the spirits give power over the contingent world, that of the Spirit and Time.

BENEDICTION OF THE CIRCLE

When the circle is perfect, you must sprinkle it with holy or purging water whilst saying the following phrase:

Asperges me, domine, hyssopo, et mundabor: lavabia, et super nivem dealbabor.

BENEDICTION OF PERFUMES

Now comes the Benediction of Perfumes, which must be spoken whilst sprinkling the perfumes with holy water:

The God of Abraham, God of Isaac, God of Jacob, bless here the creatures of these kinds, that they may fill up the power and virtue of their odours, so that neither the enemy nor any false imagination may be able to enter into them; through our Lord Jesus Christ.

EXORCISM OF FIRE

Next you must perform the Exorcism of Fire, into which the Perfumes are to be put. The fire to be used must be placed in a new clay vase. The exorcism is performed by reciting the following:

I exercise thee, O thou creature of fire, by him by whom all things are made, that forthwith thou cast away every phantasm from thee, that it shall not be able to do any hurt in anything.
Bless, O Lord, this creature of fire and sanctify it, so that it may be blessed to set forth the praise of thy holy name, and that no hurt may be permitted to come to the exorciser or spectators; through our Lord Jesus Christ.

EXORCIST'S HABIT

If possible, the habit should be a priest's garment. Failing that, you must use clean fine white linen. The following oration must be said when putting on the habit:

Ancor, Amacor, Amides, Theodonias, Anitor, by the merits of thy angels, O Lord, I will put on the garment of salvation, that this which I desire I may bring to effect through thee, the most holy Adonay, whose kingdom endureth for ever and ever. Amen.

PENTACLE

Then you must take a Pentacle (see illustration below) that has been made in the day and hour of Mercury, the Moon increasing, on a parchment made from the skin of a virgin goat, but first you must say the Mass of the Holy Ghost over it whilst sprinkling it with water of baptism.


Solomon's Pentacle

MANNER OF WORKING

Preferably wait until the Moon is increasing and equal, and let her not be combust. Be clean and pure for at least nine days before beginning the work. Go to confession and receive the holy communion. Have ready the perfume appropriated to the day on which the work is to be performed. Obtain holy water from a priest, a clay vase filled with fire, the habit and the pentacle; all of these items must be rightly and duly consecrated.
On the day of the operation, one of the companions shall carry the clay vase filled with fire, another the book, and another the habit and the pentacle. The person performing the operation shall carry a sword, over which a Mass of the Holy Ghost must be said, and in the middle of the sword, there shall be written the name Agla + and on the other side, the name + On +. When going to the consecrated place, he shall continue to read litanies and his companions shall answer him. Upon arriving at the place, let him draw the perfect circle as shown above and let him bless it.
The master, who shall be purified with fasting, chastity and abstinence from all luxury during the seven days preceding the operation, shall be clothed with the habit and enter the circle with the pentacle, perfumes and all things necessary hereunto. Turning towards the four parts of the world, he shall call the angels ruling the seven days of the week, and with bended knees calling the angels name by name, he will say:

O Angels, be my helpers in these petitions, and help me in my affairs and petitions.

Then turning towards the four parts of the world, he will call the angels ruling the air on that day, and after imploring all the names and spirits written in the circle, he shall say:

I adjure and call you all forth, by the seat of Adonay and by Hagios, O Theos, Iscyros, Athanatos, Paracletus, Alpha and Omega, and by these three secret names: Agla, On, Tetragrammaton, that you accomplish today what I desire.

When that is done, all that remains is to read the conjuration assigned for the day on which the operation is performed.

Duties of the angels

The spirits of the air of Saturday are subject to the south-west wind. Their nature is to sow discords, hatred, evil thoughts and cogitations, to give leave freely, to slay and kill every one, and to maim every member.

Familiar appearance of the spirits of Saturday, the day of Saturn

The spirits of Saturday most often appear with a tall, lean, slender body, with an angry countenance, having four faces, one on the back of the head, one in the front, both with beaks, and the two other faces on the knees. They are black in colour and transparent. Their motion is the moving of the wind, with a kind of earthquake, Their sign is earth, whiter than the snow.
