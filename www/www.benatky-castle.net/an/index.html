Benatky nad Jizeriou is a small town situated around 30 kilometres from Prague in the lower Pojizéri region and is known for its castle, which was home to Tycho Brahe in the 16th century and the Czech composer Bedrich Smetana. The town, which encompasses the three villages of Nova Benatky, Staré Benatky and Obodr, today represents a natural centre with a wealth of cultural activities.
