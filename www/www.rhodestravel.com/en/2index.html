Combining tradition and history in a luxurious natural setting ideal for taking it easy, Rhodes is perfect for tourists seeking a cultural and relaxing holiday. That is why we would like to share our favourite walks and addresses with the rest of the world, illustrated here and there with our most attractive photos.

Rhodes is situated in the Aegean sea near the Turkish coast and is the largest of the Dodecanese islands. It has benefited from the protection of the sun since mythological times. The legend goes that the island rose out of the sea when the world was divided between the different gods and was attributed to Helios. However, it owes it name to the nymph Rhodos, wife of the sun god.
The small island is littered with tributes to the sun, although hardly anything remains of its greatest monuments. The Colossus, which is the ultimate symbol of worship, has completely disappeared.
