The bookshop is located in the Beyazit district,
right in the heart of Istanbul's historic centre,
and is open for business from Monday to Saturday between 9 am and 6 pm.

Our bookshop is home to a wide range of ancient and modern calligraphies, signed by some of Turkey's greatest artists or simply left anonymous, bearing all the hallmarks of the traditional methods that have made their reputation.
With new arrivals every week, the KILIC bookshop has a major collection of ancient books. Some of our latest acquisitions include the following:
