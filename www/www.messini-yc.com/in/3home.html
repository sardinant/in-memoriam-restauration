The Vecchioni Yacht Club is located at Bacoli, a stone's throw from Naples, and benefits from the exceptional setting of the coastline of the Tyrrhenian sea.

The club was created in 1985 by the yachtsman Matteo Vecchioni and currently has a considerable fleet of monotypes, in additional to a nationwide reputation.

You can become a member straight away and take part in the courses organised by the club!
