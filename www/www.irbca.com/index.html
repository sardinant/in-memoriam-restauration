Welcome to the web site of the association for collectors of rare and antique books.
The aim of our association is to simplify contact between collectors and promote exchanges by developing a reliable network on a worldwide scale.

The association was created following an encounter between five collectors from five different continents, thereby guaranteeing effective prospecting and a tight-knit international community:

Bret Houston – USA
Hent Touimi Ben Jelloum – Morocco
Hiroki Yosunari – Japan
Jacques Moreau – France
Jessica Johnston – Australia

Represented by 25 specialist bookshops,
we encourage teamwork on an international scale
by regularly publishing an updated list of the books available on the market.
