My name is Stefan Stiegler.
I live in Berlin, Germany.
Ever since the age of 12, I've been fascinated by esotericism, and more particularly magic.

This subject is too often the source of ridicule on the Internet by so-called specialists whose knowledge is both limited and inaccurate.

I've created this site for the purpose of setting the record straight by tracing its history back to the masters of this fascinating art.
