- Naples, January 2, 2002 -

Dear Readers,

Today, after two years of online reporting, I am compelled to put an end to this blog. Taking a close interest in the shady world of the mafia and the camorra is not without risk. The law of silence has ordered me to cease all my investigations if I want to see my two daughters grow up. At least, this will have been an unforgettable experience in my career as a freelance reporter.
I want to thank all of you who helped me with your e-mails and encouragement.

Yours truly,
Dario Sabelli
