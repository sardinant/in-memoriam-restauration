+ 10 February: My regular reader
 
This will have to be quick, as he's waiting for me. We've arranged to go to the museum together at 3 o'clock. When I initially arrived at Virginia's house, I was lost as I barely knew the people there. There was no-one to speak to. Virginia just welcomed me with a smile and then turned her back on me. I'd borrowed a sheepskin coat from Miss Thomson and I felt ridiculous. Then I noticed him. He was standing out of the way of the others as well. I asked him if he was part of Virginia's family. He was a bit older than most of the people at the party. He smiled and said that he already knew who I was, as he was one of my secret readers. I went bright red. I could have died. I almost lied and said that I didn't know what he was talking about. He said that he was a lost traveller who'd stumbled upon my site like an oasis, a source of fresh water. "In fact, would you like a drink", he asked. As if by magic, he gave me a glass of champagne, which I downed in one go, given that I was feeling a bit flustered. Afterwards, I felt light-headed. I was drunk on his words. We spoke. He was incredibly cultured! Later that evening when I wanted to go home, I couldn't find Miss Thomson's bike anywhere. He told me that a lot of bikes are stolen in Oxford.
He walked me home. I asked him not to read the site anymore. He promised, provided that I'd tell him everything. I promised. What an exchange!
There we go. I'd best be off. I'll tell you the rest tomorrow.
