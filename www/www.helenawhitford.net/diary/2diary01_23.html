+ 23 January: My new room.
 
My new room's brilliant. There's a lot of natural light, and the window looks out over the small garden. Virginia helped me move along with her three brothers, John, William and Stephen, all of them well-built, dark-haired and quiet. They loaded all my boxes in the van belonging to one of Miss Thomson's friends. In two hours, we'd already finished. Virginia gave me a hand to tidy up and clean the room that I was leaving, as well as the new room I was moving into.
She was complaining, "Typical, I knew they'd disappear as soon as the tidying up needed to be done!" I was more than happy that her brothers had helped with the move. They didn't even know me. Miss Thomson made us a cup of tea. Virginia thought she was a bit odd, like a wicked witch out of some fairy tale. I feel angry with myself, because I didn't stick up for her. I suppose I'm sad that my friends didn't automatically hit it off.
