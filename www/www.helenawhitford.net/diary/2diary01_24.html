+ 24 January: A ray of sunshine.
 
I was woken up by the birds singing. The sun was shining. Virginia came by with her three brothers, all on bikes. They'd come to ask me if I fancied going for a walk along the water's edge. Miss Thomson let me borrow her bike. It was a bit small for me, so I kept careering all over the place. Even so, it was a brilliant day! Why is it that I feel so sad and annoyed at myself whenever I have to leave Virginia and her family? It's as if I'm blaming myself for wasting my time and at the same time, not knowing how to enjoy myself.
