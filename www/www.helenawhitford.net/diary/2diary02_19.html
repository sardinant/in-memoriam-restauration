+ 19 February: Like the first Christians.
 
No, I can't stop writing this diary. Typing away at the computer is like a drug. It also lets me clear my head and chill out a bit, which is no easy task as of late. Fortunately, there's Miss Thomson and her friends, who are like a real family. Virginia once asked me, "Who still believes in hell? And what about the Immaculate Conception?" I almost felt like I came from another planet in answering "Me". But it's not true. There are these people that meet in secret, a bit like the first Christians, who sometimes joke that they'll be thrown to the lions.
