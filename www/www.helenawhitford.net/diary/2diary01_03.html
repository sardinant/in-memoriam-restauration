+ 3 January: Lectures have started.
 
Since I've been in Oxford, it's worried me to see how different I am from other people of the same age. It makes me feel as though I've lived far away from it all, protected from the temptation to sin in the shelter of our family cottage. The students here don't seem to realise that they're not immortal. They're interested in all sorts of trivial things, and couldn't care less about the salvation of their souls. Even the most serious philosophical discussions end in jokes. One day, I heard someone laughing that "f Jesus came back, he'd preach on the Internet!" I was shocked to begin with, and then I realised what I had to do.
