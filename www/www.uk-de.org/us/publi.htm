OUR PUBLICATIONS

The UK-DE newsletter is published six times a year and offers readers food for thought on the often complex issues relating to peace and basic human rights. The newsletter does not have any political leanings and is quick to denounce all forms of injustice, depravity and failure to respect human dignity.

Annual subscription: € 17
