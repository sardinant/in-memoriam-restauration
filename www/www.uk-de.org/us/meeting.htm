MEETING US

The Inflexible Church is made up of:

  • Independent national divisions
  • Local groups
  • Affiliated organisations
  • Corresponding members

Our Church is active in 15 countries spanning five continents, with currently more than 30 000 members.

Although the independence of the divisions is a characteristic of the movement's organisation, the international dimension is more particularly prevalent in the:

  • Theme-based committees
    • Alternatives to violence
    • Resolving conflicts and mediation
    • Faith, dialogue and reconciliation
    • The spirituality of peace
    • Education on peace
  • Regional work groups
    • Africa
    • Asia - Pacific
    • Latin America and the Caribbean
    • Middle East
    • Central and Eastern Europe
    • Balkans and Baltic States
    • Commonwealth countries

Every two years, an International Council brings together the delegates from each member country.

The activities and international organisation of the movement are coordinated by our secretariat in Brussels.
