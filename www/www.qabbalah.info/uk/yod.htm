Bibliographical sources

Mystères de la Kabbale
Marc-Alain Ouaknin (Publisher: Abbeville Press, 2000)

"Woe betide me if I reveal these mysteries and woe betide me if I do not", so it is written in the Zohar, the major work in Jewish mysticism. The Kabbalah is the secret esoteric face of Judaism, whereas the Bible represents the exposed face. An immense universe to which Marc-Alain Ouaknin opens the door: basic diagrams of the way in which the world is organised, structure of primordial man, meditation on the letters of the alphabet and the names of God, perception of the "light-life-energy" that courses through everything, as well as historical overviews, parallels between the Kabbalah and therapy, suggested exercises for meditation, and so on.
Quotes, anecdotes, diagrams, illustrations (in black and white), a wealth of subtitles, an uncluttered layout, sober yet elegant prose, clear typography with an interesting play between black and sepia... everything has been done to stimulate the reading process and simplify the approach to an undoubtedly difficult subject. (Colette-Rebecca Estin - amazon.fr)

Initiation à la Kabbale hébraïque
Adolphe D. Grad (Publisher: Rocher, 2001)

The Kabbalah... a mysterious word perpetuated by the Hebrews that opens the way to a spirituality overflowing the bed of Hebraism to reach the universal.
Adolphe D. Grad, one of the foremost authorities on the Kabbalah, has penned a fascinating initiation into the key themes of this sacred science, including the Bible, Zohar, the Song of Solomon, circumcision, virginity, golems, magic squares... Just some of the clues and markers on the path of the Kabbalah. (Rocher)

La Kabbale
Gershom Scholem (Gallimard - Folio Essais, 2003)

In this book, Gershom Scholem provides all the keys required to understand the Kabbalah, the mystical movement dating back to ancient times that took on its final shape in the 20th century. The concepts are presented with a surprising level of clarity, despite the complexity of the works and themes addressed. Consequently, this book provides a thorough account of the knowledge on Jewish mysticism.
This work invites the reader to embark on a journey and discover Judaism's quest for freedom where the concern to respect tradition never closes on itself, but opens onto a world where utopia is present. (Gallimard)


Internet links

Journal of Kabbalah Studies

The Journal of Kabbalah Studies is an online review dedicated to research on the Kabbalah, both ancient and contemporary. It hosts a wealth of articles, accounts on various works, a wide range of bibliographical resources and archives of recently published paper-based texts and works by research students (dissertations, theses). It publishes announcements of colloquia, university seminars and events in research life. The review can be accessed anywhere without any need for a password. (Editorial committee)

Online Kabbalah

This portal is dedicated to the Kabbalah and is designed as a place for exchanges, comments, study and knowledge-sharing in an enjoyable and laid-back atmosphere. Our aim is to use the Internet to allow the possibility of interacting with the texts, commenting on them, rereading them or even re-telling them, in order to derive a meaning that goes beyond the meaning. Our wish is also to share our Love for the Kabbalah through the most modern means possible. It is open to everyone, whether beginners or advanced students. Every question is a breeding ground for an infinite number of questions; there are no "stupid questions", just silences that kill reflection... (Prospero, webmaster)
