Summer 1996
The band is created by Laurent (drums), Alex (guitars) and Pascal (bass). The band is named FalkirK. 

1997
First shows and first songs written. The bands becomes better and better and needs a full line-up to grow up. Many singers and guitarists join the band, but no one fits well enough.

Summer 1998
Stéphane and Arnaud join the band. They already are friends and want to stay together.

March 1999
1st demo recorded in 2 days with Armando Ferreira (ex H-Bomb). Great feedbacks from press. The band makes about 400 copies, all sold out.

November 1999
 Recording of "The Day Will Come" 1st album in a week, and live shows.

September 2000
"The Day Will Come" is released

Very enthousiastic reactions from press, radios....many shows performed. The band becomes more famous in France. Relationship problems with Pascal. He leaves the band in the beginning of 2001 just after a metal festival in Paris.

Fall of 2001 to Fall of 2002
Many shows everywhere in France. Songwriting of the new album.
Deal with Brennus / Musea for distribution. Some friends of the band plays bass during live shows, but FalkirK is composed by 4 people at this time.

February 2003
"Magnus Imperium" is released. Great reactions from press, radios....Didier (bass) definitely joins the band.

2003
Many shows in France to come...The album is a success in France. During this, the songwriting of the 3rd album is on its way.

2004 - 2005
New songs written,live shows and recording of the 3rd album.

2006
Gates of Dawn, the 3 rd album is released on February the 28 th, and distributed in France and Europe. New bass player, coming from detah metal scene, Guillaume joins the band. Jen also joins the band as a manager.
